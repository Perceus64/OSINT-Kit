Footprint

See what the open internet already knows.

Map a domain's public footprint, read the metadata hiding in files, and pull indicators out of threat reports. Analysis runs in your browser on Rust compiled to WebAssembly; files you drop here never leave your machine.

DNS and email security, registration record, and every subdomain that ever appeared in a public TLS certificate.

Drop files here, or choose them

Photos, PDFs, Office documents, executables, anything. You'll get hashes, the real file type, hidden metadata like GPS and author names, and any links or addresses buried inside. Nothing is uploaded.

Use this on things you own or are authorised to assess. Lookups use public sources: Google and Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS, RDAP, crt.sh certificate transparency and Team Cymru IP-to-ASN.
